Setting up

Where do I put the phone?

Lay the phone screen-up on a firm stack of books, with the camera end sticking out over the middle of your sheet. The setup check shows a dashed outline: raise the phone until the whole sheet fits inside it.

Prefer no stand? Use the lightbox: lay thin paper on the screen and trace through it. It suits drawings up to the size of your screen.

What does the level show?

The tilt of the phone from flat, in degrees. Within 1° it turns green. If your desk slopes, lay the phone flat on your paper and use Calibrate level on paper in More; Reset level clears it.

The lines look blurry

Tap Refocus in More, or raise the phone 2–3 cm. Focus locks when you lock the picture, so a hand passing under the camera does not pull it away.

It is too dark to see my pencil lines

A desk lamp helps most. The torch works too, on phones that have one. If the screen looks yellow, turn off Night Shift for an even white.

The marks

How do the marks work?

Before you lock the picture, Pencilight places two + marks in the margin of your sheet. You pencil a light cross and its letter under each one. When the phone or the paper moves, tap Re-align: drag each + back onto its pencil cross, use the 3× magnifier for the last bit, and tap Apply. The lines move back over your drawing.

To practise without paper, open How the marks work from Drawings or Settings.

Two marks or four?

Two marks bring back the position, the size and the turn. Four marks also correct a phone that tilts. Spread the marks toward the corners: marks close together give a rougher result.

A + won’t sit on its cross

Check the letters: A on the A cross, B on the B cross. If the app says the size changed a lot, the phone has probably moved up or down; put it back at its old height and try again.

The crosses are still on my drawing

They are light pencil in the margin. Erase them when you finish.

The screen reacts to my hand in the lightbox

Lock the picture first: the slide lock needs a hold before the slide, so a resting palm does nothing. Swipes from the very edge of the screen still reach iOS. Guided Access blocks them: turn it on in Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access, then triple-click the side button while you draw.

Drawings

Do I lose my place if I stop?

No. Every session saves itself, including the marks and where the picture sits. Open the drawing from Drawings and check that each + still sits on its cross.

Moving to a new phone

Your drawings live on your phone. Restoring a backup of your old phone (iCloud Backup, a computer backup or Quick Start) brings them along. Pencilight Pro comes back with Restore purchases.

Purchases

Restore a purchase

In Pencilight, open Settings and tap Restore purchases, or tap Restore at the top of the purchase screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought with; if you bought with another one, sign in with it in iOS Settings and try again.

Cancel the monthly subscription

Open Settings in Pencilight and tap Manage subscription, or go to iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the next renewal to stop that charge. Your drawings stay on your phone either way.

Switch from monthly to lifetime

In Settings, tap Switch to lifetime and buy it. Then cancel the monthly plan in Settings › your name › Subscriptions, so it stops renewing. Buying lifetime does not cancel it for you.

Ask for a refund

Apple handles refunds: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. I cannot see your payment or issue refunds myself.

A purchase is waiting for approval

With Ask to Buy, the request goes to the family organizer. Tracing unlocks as soon as it is approved; you do not need to buy again. Family Sharing is not offered for Pencilight Pro.

Language and privacy

Change the app’s language

Pencilight uses the language you pick for it in iOS Settings › Pencilight › Language.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Lines are made on your phone, and the app has no account, ads, analytics or tracking. The only thing that leaves your phone is what an App Store purchase needs. The privacy policy has the details.

I heard a shutter sound

In some countries iOS always plays the shutter sound. It is the still photo Pencilight takes for the magnifier or the finished drawing. It is kept with that drawing and never uploaded.
