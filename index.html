Your photo, traced onto paper

Pencilight turns your own photo into clean lines and shows them over the live camera view of your paper, so you can trace them with a pencil.

Paper moved? Line it up again.

  1. Pencil two light crosses in the margin of your sheet, under the app’s two + marks.
  2. When the phone or the paper gets bumped, tap Re-align. Pencilight holds a still photo of your sheet.
  3. Drag each + back onto its pencil cross, using the 3× magnifier for the last bit, and tap Apply. The lines move back over your drawing.

Four marks also correct a phone that tilts. The first screen of the app lets you try all this on a practice sheet, with no camera and no paper.

What else is in it

  • Lines you choose: Outline, Balanced or Detailed, then fewer or more lines.
  • A setup check that shows whether the whole sheet fits, and a level for a flat phone.
  • A slide lock that needs a hold before the slide, so a resting palm moves nothing.
  • Opacity, a grid, the torch, and Peek to see your own pencil lines.
  • Every session saves itself. Stop anytime and pick up where you left off.
  • A lightbox: lay thin paper on the screen and trace through it.
  • A before-and-after picture of every finished drawing.

Private by design

Lines are made on your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your photos are never uploaded. The details are in the privacy policy.

Pencilight is for iPhone. Tracing needs Pencilight Pro, a monthly subscription or a one-time purchase.
