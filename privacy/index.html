Privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026. Applies to the Pencilight app for iPhone and to this website.

The short version. Pencilight has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your photos and drawings are processed on your phone and are never uploaded by the app. The only information that leaves your phone is what an App Store purchase needs: Apple handles the payment, and RevenueCat checks whether your purchase is active.

Who is responsible

Pencilight is made by Anil Sancar, an independent developer. In this policy, “I” and “me” mean me, and “you” means you. I decide how the data described here is handled. You can write to me at anilsancarr@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Pencilight works on your phone. I run no server that could receive your photos, and the app has no code that sends them anywhere.

  • Photos you choose. The iOS photo picker gives Pencilight only the photo you pick; the app cannot see the rest of your library. A copy of that photo is kept in Pencilight’s own storage, with the drawing it belongs to. If you use Paste, the app reads the clipboard only when you tap the system Paste button.
  • Lines. The lines are made from your photo on the phone itself.
  • The camera. The live view shows your paper with your lines on top. Pencilight takes a still photo of your sheet only when you check or re-align your marks and when you finish a drawing. Those stills are kept with that drawing so it can be resumed later.
  • Before-and-after pictures. They are saved to your Photos library only when you tap Save, with add-only access, and shared only where you send them with Share.
  • Motion sensors. The phone’s tilt is read on the phone to show the level. If you calibrate the level, the reference direction is stored.
  • Settings. Things like which tips you have seen, your level calibration, a trial reminder you asked for, and a copy of your last purchase status, so that tracing still opens when you are offline.
  • Reminders. The optional trial reminder is a local notification scheduled on your phone. Pencilight does not use push notifications.

Deleting it. Deleting a drawing removes its photo, lines, stills and marks. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Backups. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your phone to a computer, iOS may include Pencilight’s data in that backup, under Apple’s terms. That is why I say your photos are never uploaded by Pencilight, rather than promising they can never leave your phone.

What leaves your phone: purchases

Tracing needs Pencilight Pro, sold through the App Store. Two companies take part.

Apple

Apple processes every payment, trial and renewal. I never see your name, your card or your Apple Account. Apple’s handling of that data is described in Apple’s privacy policy.

RevenueCat

Pencilight uses RevenueCat, Inc. (United States) to confirm purchases, restore them, and tell the app whether Pencilight Pro is active. RevenueCat acts on my behalf as a data processor. When the app starts, and when you buy or restore, the RevenueCat software in the app sends RevenueCat:

  • a random identifier that RevenueCat creates for this installation of the app; it is not your name, email or Apple Account;
  • your Pencilight purchase history from the App Store: products, purchase and renewal dates, prices and currency, store country, and trial and renewal status;
  • technical details that come with the request: the app and iOS versions, the device model, your preferred languages, a device identifier that is the same only across apps from the same developer (Apple’s “identifier for vendor”), and your IP address.

I use this to give you what you bought, to prevent purchase fraud, and to see sales totals in RevenueCat’s dashboard. It is not linked to your identity, and it is not used for advertising or to track you across apps or websites. RevenueCat’s own practices are in RevenueCat’s privacy policy. RevenueCat keeps purchase records for as long as I use its service, and deletes them when I ask.

What Pencilight does not do

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier. There is nothing to ask permission to track for, so the app never shows the tracking prompt.
  • No analytics or usage statistics.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

If you have turned on “Share With App Developers” in iOS Settings, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports. That is Apple’s choice to offer and yours to make, and you can turn it off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Legal bases

For people in the EU, the UK and similar places: purchase records are processed to provide the purchase you asked for (performing a contract) and to prevent fraud and understand sales (legitimate interests). RevenueCat processes them in the United States under its data processing terms with me.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data, or object to how it is used. Because there is no account and I hold no copy of your photos, most of this is already in your hands: delete a drawing, or delete the app.

For purchase records at RevenueCat, write to me with the Order ID from Apple’s receipt email. I will find the matching record and give you a copy or have it deleted. Deleting it does not cancel a subscription; you cancel that in your Apple Account settings. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Pencilight is made for adults who draw and is not directed at children under 13. Apart from the purchase records above, it collects no personal data from anyone.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Its fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers when you open a page. The company that hosts the site may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date. If a change affects what leaves your phone, I will say so in the app’s release notes.
